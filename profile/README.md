# ARTISAN GitHub Organization

## Empowering Scientific Discovery with Computational Science and AI Innovations

Welcome to the GitHub organization for ARTISAN (Center for Artificial Intelligence in Science and Engineering) at the Georgia Institute of Technology (Georgia Tech). ARTISAN leverages Georgia Tech’s distinguished strengths in computational science, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and interdisciplinary research to accelerate advancements across diverse scientific and engineering disciplines.

### About ARTISAN

ARTISAN develops open-source platforms, tools, and methodologies designed to enhance computational research capabilities for scientists and engineers at Georgia Tech and worldwide. Our ecosystem promotes collaborative innovation and supports the integration of cutting-edge AI techniques in scientific workflows.

### Project Highlights

#### Cybershuttle

Cybershuttle is a state-of-the-art computational science framework that seamlessly integrates local and remote computing resources. Its primary objective is to reduce friction when researchers transition from interactive, local computational environments to powerful, scalable remote infrastructure.

##### Key Features:

* **Local-First Computing:** Allows users to seamlessly scale from local workstations to remote computational clusters and cloud services.
* **Interactive and Scalable:** Balances intuitive, interactive local computing with large-scale remote high-performance computational power.
* **Open and Modular:** Supports extensibility and customization through modular design, enabling easy integration of domain-specific AI and computational tools.

Explore Cybershuttle: [Cybershuttle GitHub](https://github.com/GeorgiaTech-ARTISAN/cybershuttle)
